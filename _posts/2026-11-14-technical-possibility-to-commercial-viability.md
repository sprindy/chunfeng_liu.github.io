My AI Jargon 101 series explains the concepts; The Five-Layer AI Cake explores the layers that make AI possible. This series examines their business implications and strategic choices.

An impressive AI demo shows what is possible. Lasting business value takes more.

## 1. From prototype to commercial viability
{: #section-1-from-prototype-to-commercial-viability }

Many innovations develop through imagination, scientific investigation, proof of concept and working prototypes.

Commercialization adds four practical tests:

- 💡 **Useful**: Does it solve a meaningful problem?
- 💰 **Affordable**: Do the economics work for customers and suppliers?
- 🧩 **Suitable**: Does it fit workflows, user needs, safety and reliability requirements?
- 🌱 **Sustainable**: Can it remain financially, operationally and environmentally viable?

Steam engines, electric power systems and the internet show why practical applications and supporting infrastructure matter.

These stages overlap. Customer feedback also shapes technical improvements.

For AI, assess the whole workflow: quality, integration, human review, maintenance and total cost.

## 2. How data, algorithms, and compute come together
{: #section-2-how-data-algorithms-and-compute-come-together }

New capabilities can emerge by combining existing and new advances. Modern AI brings together:

- 📚 **Data**: Recorded information: numbers, text, images, audio, video and sensor readings. <https://lnkd.in/p/gk5xtqfp>
- 🧠 **Algorithms and models**: Methods that turn data into useful capabilities. <https://lnkd.in/p/gh-Gfk9Z>
- ⚡ **Compute**: Processing power, memory and infrastructure for training and inference. <https://lnkd.in/p/g4cf4hGH>

{% include perspective-figure.html src="/assets/blog/ai-in-perspective/technical-possibility-to-commercial-viability-2.jpg" alt="Three pillars of modern AI: data, algorithms and models, and computing infrastructure." caption="The three foundations of modern AI" %}

AlexNet’s 2012 breakthrough illustrates this combination: ImageNet data + a deep neural network + GPU computing.

{% include perspective-figure.html src="/assets/blog/ai-in-perspective/technical-possibility-to-commercial-viability-3.jpg" alt="Timeline of selected AI milestones from Turing and Dartmouth through AlexNet, ChatGPT, and agentic workflows." caption="AI milestones from early research to agentic workflows" %}

Each foundation continues to evolve. Better algorithms can use computing resources more efficiently; better data can improve results; better hardware can make new applications practical.

**My strategic takeaway**: evaluate the whole combination—and the conditions needed to turn it into value.
