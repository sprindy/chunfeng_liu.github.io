## The problem

Supporting new motion, magnetic, and pressure sensors meant repeating integration work across variants. The team needed a way to ship new drivers faster while keeping diagnostics and verification consistent.

## My role

I owned the Qualcomm sensor driver architecture. I designed a modular framework for the Sensor Execution Environment (SEE) and standardized driver templates, CI, and diagnostics. The shared framework gave engineers a consistent starting point and let them concentrate on the parts specific to each device.

## Outcome

New sensor SKU onboarding took about half as long. Standardized CI and diagnostics also helped reduce integration defects and debugging cycles by approximately 30%.
