## The work

At HTC, I developed sensing and low-power firmware on STM32 and FreeRTOS for VR controllers. I also worked on Android platforms, where I built diagnostic tools for Qualcomm- and MediaTek-based smartphones and investigated device reliability issues.

## What I brought to the work

I was comfortable tracing an issue across firmware, drivers, and physical measurements. That experience carried into later platform work, where diagnosing an integration issue often meant bridging the same boundaries across teams.
