## The problem

Getting a sensor through platform certification requires engineering, verification, and customer-facing teams to work from the same list of risks. A technical failure can require action from several teams before testing can resume.

## My role

I led work across driver development, verification, and customer support. I coordinated testing and debugging across regions and communicated technical risks and mitigation options to OEM stakeholders. During the Tier 1 effort, I helped secure design documentation and debugging expertise from teams outside my own.

## Outcome

The work delivered Qualcomm Tier 1 certification for three IMU variants across two series and Tier 2 certification for more than 20 variants across six series.
