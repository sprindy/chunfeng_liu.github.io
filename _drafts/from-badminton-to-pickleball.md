I organized a badminton club from 2021 to 2025 and started playing pickleball in 2025. These days, badminton and pickleball are my regular games, with some tennis and table tennis when I get the chance.

## A different rhythm

Pickleball doubles has a distinct pace, especially when both teams are close to the net. A soft shot can keep an opponent from attacking and buy time for a partner to get into position. In badminton doubles, changing the pace can serve a similar purpose, even though the court and the shots feel quite different.

That’s part of the appeal of picking up another racket sport. I get to bring experience from one game and spend time learning the patterns of another.
